The Story of Shahr-e-Khaas

Born in the Heart of Downtown Srinagar

In Khoja Bazar, food is not a business — it's a living street culture. Efren was created to bring high-fire charcoal grilling, hearty burgers, and crispy momos to our historic neighborhood.

✦
Chapter I · The Fire & The Street

The Grill Right At The Counter

Downtown Srinagar (Shahr-e-Khaas) has always been the culinary soul of Kashmir. Walk through Khoja Bazar at twilight, and you will hear skewers sizzling over glowing embers, smelling the rich roasted spice smoke that drifts past centuries-old brickwork.

We built Efren to celebrate that exact energy. We placed our live charcoal grill right at the storefront order counter. You can see your Chicken Roast turning golden brown and hear your Beef Steak sear the moment it hits the blazing cast-iron grate.

“Ek Baar Khao Ge, Baar Baar Aao Ge. When the charcoal is hot and the spices are true, you never forget the taste.”
Chapter II · The Vibe

Small Scale, Immense Flavor

We keep things intimate on purpose. Efren is a small-scale, cozy neighborhood bistro where every plate is made to order. No industrial warming trays, no compromise on freshness.

Grab a seat with your friends at our wooden tables inside, or order your meal ready-to-eat to take home. From ₹69 crispy fries and ₹70 BBQ skewers to gourmet Beef Cheeseburgers and ₹79 craft mojitos, great food in Downtown is made for everyone.
