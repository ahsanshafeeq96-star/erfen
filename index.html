Where are you, Downtown? Right in Khoja Bazar.

Tucked into the historic lanes of Chinkral Mohalla in Khoja Bazar, Efren is an intimate neighborhood gem. You can smell the charcoal fire and roasted spices from the street before you spot our glowing Corten sign.

We aren't a massive banquet hall; we are a cozy, warm, ready-to-eat and dine-in bistro. Grab a rustic wooden table with friends, watch the grillmaster turn sizzling beef skewers over red-hot coals, or pick up a piping-hot meal on your way home.
