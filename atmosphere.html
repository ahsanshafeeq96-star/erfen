A Downtown Srinagar Gathering Spot

Cozy, Intimate & Alive with Fire

An unpretentious neighborhood bistro in Khoja Bazar. Small-scale, warm, and built around sizzling live charcoal where you can dine in comfort or pick up ready-to-eat favorites.

✦
Experience I · The Live Counter

Sizzling Embers & Ready-To-Eat Delights

The moment you walk up to our wooden counter in Khoja Bazar, you are greeted by the warmth of real charcoal flame. Our cooks turn sizzling skewers of spiced beef, flame-roast seasoned chickens, and grill juicy cheeseburgers right before your eyes.

It's fast, fresh, and bursting with flavor. Grab a stool at the counter, order your meal, and watch the sparks fly while your food is prepared in minutes.

Check Counter Menu ✦ READY-TO-EAT · PACKED FRESH
Experience II · Dine-In Comfort

The Cozy Brick & Timber Room

Step inside into our comfortable dine-in space: exposed vintage brick, warm Edison bulb chandeliers, and sturdy dark wood tables made for sharing plates.

Whether it’s friends sharing a basket of hot Kurkure Momos and chilled Mojitos or families savoring our sizzling Beef Steak and whole Chicken Roast, our small dining room is always filled with laughter, conversation, and the comforting smell of roasted spices.

Save a Table ✦ COZY SEATING FOR 20-25 PATRONS
Experience III · The Street Corner

The Heart of Khoja Bazar, Downtown

Our storefront sits at the bustling intersection of Chinkral Mohalla in Khoja Bazar. With its vintage deodar wood frame and glowing Corten steel sign, Efren is a welcoming beacon in the evening dusk.

Stop by for a quick takeaway parcel or pull up a chair inside. Open seven days a week from 11:30 AM to late night.
